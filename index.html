About Sadhana Foundation

Sadhana Foundation is a non-profit social welfare organization committed to creating positive change in society through humanitarian service, education support, and community development initiatives. Founded with the vision of helping underprivileged communities, the foundation works tirelessly to bring hope, dignity, and opportunities to those in need.


Our primary focus is to support economically disadvantaged families, children, and elderly citizens by providing essential resources, educational materials, and seasonal necessities. Through various outreach programs, we aim to reduce hardship and improve the quality of life for vulnerable sections of society.


On 16 January 2026, Sadhana Foundation organized a Winter Distribution Drive at Belpahari, Jhargram, where blankets, sweaters, jackets, combs, and other winter essentials were distributed among needy families. During the same initiative, school bags, notebooks, pencils, and educational materials were provided to children to encourage learning and support their academic growth.


Continuing our mission of service, on 26 January 2026, the foundation conducted another donation drive at Mukutmanipur. Winter clothing and other essential belongings were distributed among local residents, helping them cope with the cold season and improving their daily living conditions.


At Sadhana Foundation, we believe that even small acts of kindness can create meaningful change. Through the dedication of our volunteers, donors, and supporters, we strive to build stronger communities and a brighter future for generations to come.

Our Mission

To empower underprivileged communities through education, social welfare, humanitarian assistance, and community-driven development initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate and inclusive society where every individual has access to basic necessities, educational opportunities, and a life of dignity.

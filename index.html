About Sadhana Foundation

Sadhana Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to serving underprivileged communities through social welfare programs, educational support and humanitarian aid. Our mission is to bring hope, dignity and opportunities to those in need.

We regularly organize winter clothing distribution drives, educational material donation programs and community development initiatives across West Bengal.

Recent Activities

Winter Distribution Drive

Date: 16 January 2026

Location: Belpahari, Jhargram

Distribution of winter essentials including jackets, sweaters, combs and other necessities. School bags, pencils and notebooks were also donated to children.

Republic Day Charity Drive

Date: 26 January 2026

Location: Mukutmanipur

Distribution of winter clothing and essential belongings to support needy families and elderly citizens during the winter season.
